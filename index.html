What does Buy Here Pay Here mean?

It means the dealership finances your car itself. You choose a vehicle, set up a payment plan with us, and make your payments directly to Buy Here Pay Here NJ instead of a bank or outside lender.

Can I get approved with bad credit?

Bad credit is not an automatic no here. We specialize in second chance financing and look at your overall situation, not only your credit score. Approval depends on your circumstances, so call 973-524-7694 to talk it through.

What kinds of affordable cars do you sell?

We sell quality pre-owned vehicles at budget-friendly prices, including sedans, SUVs, and everyday commuters. Our selection changes often, so call or stop by to see what is available.

What should I bring when I come in?

Bring a valid driver's license and, if you can, proof of income and proof of address. Requirements can vary, so call ahead and we will tell you exactly what to have with you.
